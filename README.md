# McCoy's Lab

Health-monitoring software for astronauts on space missions.

Built for the **NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026** — Challenge #4: Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions.

Team **USS Enterprise** — solo, by Izabel Passos.

## What it does

McCoy's Lab helps an astronaut assess their own health, with no doctor on board. It tracks **resting heart rate** over time, links it to **sleep and recovery**, and gives clear guidance when something looks off.

## How it works

- **Day 1 — Space station.** Log your heart rate, see it charted over time, and get an alert after **1 day** off your baseline. First advice: **rest and re-measure**.
- **Day 2 — Spacecraft.** The same flow, adapted to the spacecraft environment.
- **Stretch — Rover.** If time remains.

## Why heart rate first

Resting heart rate is simple to measure in space, it moves with sleep and recovery, and it is the earliest signal in the flow. One indicator, end to end, before adding more.
